When does it start?

On December 1. The first 100 on the waitlist get in on November 24. Everyone who signs up gets an email with the link to the app on launch day.

What happens to my email?

We send you the confirmation, the cheat sheet and, at launch, the link to the app. No newsletter spam, no sharing. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Do I need sales experience?

No. Sales Universe is written for beginners. Jargon is explained, and the learning path starts with the basics.

How much time do I need each day?

You decide with your daily goal: from half a lesson to three lessons and more. Your streak counts every day you hit your goal.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. It needs the internet once to unlock, then it runs offline.

On how many devices can I learn?

The annual pass works on your iPhone and your Mac with the same Apple ID. Link your devices in the settings and your progress is the same everywhere.

Does the app store anything about my customers?

No. Sales Universe is not a CRM. It only stores your own progress, your own notes and your review after a call, on your device. If you turn on sync between phone and computer, they’re encrypted on your device first. We can’t read them.

What happens after the 30 days?

The trial ends on its own, and nothing is charged. If you get the annual pass, you keep learning with your progress so far.
